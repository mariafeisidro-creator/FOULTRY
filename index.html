About Our Poultry Farm

Mariafe's Poultry Farm is a family-run poultry business dedicated to raising healthy, high-quality chickens of various ages and breeds. We believe every customer deserves strong, vibrant birds at fair prices.

Locally sourced breeds include Rhode Island Red, Australorp, Plymouth Rock, and native chickens. Visit us and see the difference quality makes.
